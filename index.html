Fetch songs
Pull tracks out of the music catalogue to top up a playlist — an artist, a band, a genre word, anything. Everything arrives with a real release year you check before adding.
Import a song list
Paste one song per line — Artist - Title is ideal, but rough YouTube titles are cleaned up automatically. Each line is looked up against iTunes and then Deezer to get a clean title, artist, release year and a playable clip. No account, no API key.
▸ How to get the list out of a YouTube playlist
YouTube gives no way to read a playlist without a developer key, so grab the titles from the page instead. Open the playlist in your browser, scroll to the bottom so every video has loaded, press F12 → Console, paste this and hit enter. The song list is copied to your clipboard — then paste it in the box above. 
copy([...document.querySelectorAll('#video-title')]
  .map(e => e.textContent.trim())
  .filter(Boolean).join('\n'))
